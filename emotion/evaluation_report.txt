NOTES / LIMITATIONS (see also README.md and Part 31)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- This dataset is small (a few dozen examples per class). These
  numbers describe how this specific model performs on this
  specific held-out split - they are NOT a general claim about
  real-world accuracy on unseen users' phrasing.
- The model is NOT clinically validated and does not diagnose
  any mental-health condition. It classifies expressed emotional
  language only.
- CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD (config.py) is an application-level
  decision threshold, not a clinical confidence measure.
